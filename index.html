Hello Washington!

A page about our home planet

Washington is the Evergreen State and part of the PNW. It is known for hiking, and fresh apples.

Washington is about 136 years old . The capitol is Olympia, Its main airport is Seattle Tacoma International airport, and 8 million people live there.

Three facts about Washington

  • It is the only state named after a president.
  • It is home to the Boeing Everett Factory.
  • It is home to the only temperate rainforests in the continental United States.
